Privacy Policy

Sesame Bloom: Idle Healer · Last updated: 20 October 2026

This policy explains what information the game Sesame Bloom: Idle Healer (the "Game"), published by Sesame Peanut ("we"), handles and why. We keep it short because the Game collects very little.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (stages, heroes, items, settings) and a local gameplay log used to improve game balance are saved only on your device. We do not receive this information. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its data deletes it.

Google Play Games (sign-in and cloud save)

If you sign in with Google Play Games, Google provides the Game with your Play Games player ID and display name, and your game progress is saved to your Google account through Google's Saved Games service so you can continue on another device. This data is stored by Google, not by us, under the Google Privacy Policy. You can play without signing in. You can delete saved game data from the Google Play Games app or your Google account settings.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game offers optional rewarded ads: you choose to watch an ad to receive an in-game bonus. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and some US states), the Game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

What we do not do

  • We do not run our own servers that collect your data.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • The Game has no chat, no user accounts of its own and no real-money purchases.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Security

Data sent to Google services is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
